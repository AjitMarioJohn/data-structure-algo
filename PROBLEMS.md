## How to run it

1. Node in Tone 1/2 → solve that pattern's ⭐ list, TIMED, in Java, in repo.
2. Log every over-time / failed problem as weak spot in `game_state.md`.
3. Redo failures: +3 days, then +2 weeks (spaced repetition).
4. Extended problems: between nodes, raid prep, or when streak feels cheap.
5. After Phase 6, start weekly "random 2": two random ⭐ from cleared patterns, timed. Interviews are blind; practice blind.

## Counts

Core ⭐: ~175 · Extended: ~110 · Total: ~285 (few deliberate re-solves across patterns).
Clear all ⭐ timed + hold your own in Tone 3 fights = Google phone-screen ready.
Clear extended too = onsite ready.
